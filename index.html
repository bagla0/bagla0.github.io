About

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Aerospace Engineering at Purdue University, working with Prof. Wenbin Yu at the Composites Manufacturing and Simulation Center. I expect to graduate in May 2027. Before Purdue I did my M.Tech. at IIT Kanpur and worked as a structural design engineer on unmanned aircraft.

Composite structures are built from many thin layers of fiber and resin. Simulating every layer of a 100-meter blade in full 3-D takes days on a supercomputer. My research finds a shortcut that keeps the accuracy: model a small, representative piece of the structure in detail, compute from it the properties a designer needs for a beam, a plate or a solid part, run that fast model, and then go back to find the stress inside each layer.

I lead the development of OpenSG, the open-source software that does this. It started in 2023 as a wind-turbine-blade project with Sandia National Laboratories and NREL. Today it is an NSF-funded, GPU-accelerated code developed with the Air Force Research Laboratory. I also build AI agents that run these analyses for engineers on the CompositesAI platform.

What I work on

Three connected pieces: the models, the application that drove them, and the software that makes them fast.

Fast, accurate models of composite parts

Beam, plate and solid models that match full 3-D finite element results while running in seconds, with the stresses inside each layer recovered afterwards. Verified against VABS, SwiftComp, ANSYS and Abaqus.

Wind turbine blades

Real blades taper, twist and curve, which simpler methods ignore. With Sandia and NREL I built a pipeline from the blade geometry to a validated beam model, to the stresses inside the blade, and to buckling checks. Tested on 100-meter-class blades, including the IEA 15 MW and 22 MW reference designs.

GPU simulation and AI agents

I rewrote OpenSG in JAX so it runs on GPUs, computes exact design sensitivities automatically and connects to machine-learning models. I also built AI agents that build the mesh, run the analysis and compare designs on the CompositesAI platform.

OpenSG

Free, open-source software for multiscale analysis of composite structures. Apache-2.0 license.

You give OpenSG a detailed model of a representative piece of a structure. It returns the engineering properties of the whole part and the stresses inside every layer.

What it computes

  • Beam properties for blades, wings and tapered segments
  • Plate and shell properties for laminates, sandwich panels and lattice cores
  • 3-D material properties for unit cells, lattices and metamaterials
  • Stresses, strains and displacements inside each layer, for all of the above
